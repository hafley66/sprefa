## Laws

- `cargo test` (10 suites now) stays green with byte-identical stdout on every oracle
  case. Tracing writes to stderr only. If a test compares stderr, read it before
  touching the line it compares.
- No `eprintln!` remains in `src/**` except lines that print the JSON result to stdout
  are `println!`; every stderr line goes through `tracing`. Grep at the end:
  `grep -rn "eprintln!" v8/src | wc -l` must print `0`.
- `#[tracing::instrument]` always with `skip_all`; never let a `Universe` or a `Vec<TermId>`
  be formatted into a span field. Fields are counts, names, paths, outcomes.
- Every span name is the folder name (`read`, `macrotime`, `lower`, `check`, `comptime`,
  `reify`, `eval`). Every event target starts with `dl8::`.
- Files `_<n>_name.rs`; no new files under `src/` except none are needed. Tests only in
  `tests/`. Everything `pub`. No `unsafe`. No statics beyond what `tracing` macros make.
- `cargo clippy --all-targets` zero warnings. `cargo fmt` once before commit.
- Banned words in prose and identifiers: provenance, substrate, load-bearing, regime,
  refusal, "ground truth". No em dashes.
- 10 s cap on every command. The whole suite ran in under 15 s before you started.
