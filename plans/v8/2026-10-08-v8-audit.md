## 6. Read of direction

Three products are being built at once, each with its own engine:

| product | repo | engine |
|---|---|---|
| fact extraction (cst, types, calls, df) | hafley_scm, sprefa-extract, ryi | scm++ + RA / tsc / scip |
| relational query + IVM over facts | sqlite_ivm, ivm-dd | sqlite_ivm, DD, (duckdb/openivm asked 10-05) |
| a language (dl7) with comptime/macrotime/emit | sprefa v8 | its own lowering onto the above |

The 10-05 quote assigns roles: ryi = one-repo analysis, sprefa = cross-repo join.
The 09-21 and 10-03 quotes ask whether the middle row is a library (pgq / duckdb / substrait)
instead of a build. The third row depends on the middle row's IR (`ivm_ir`), so every engine
change in section 2 reached into v8 lowering.
