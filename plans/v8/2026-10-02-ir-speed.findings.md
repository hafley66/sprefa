# Findings: comptime cost on ir:sqlite and ir:dd (2026-10-02, base c2b318280 + sqlite_ivm 4bb8440)

Workload: `dl8 build std/tsi_registry_emit.dl7`, debug profile (`opt-level 1`, deps 3), output
`cmp`-identical to `hafley_scm/src/read/tsi/registry.rs` on both engines.

| engine | wall | macrotime phase | comptime phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| ir:sqlite | 8.24 s | 2423 ms | 5429 ms |
| ir:dd | 5.67 s | 1226 ms | 4233 ms |

Method: `sample` (macOS) call graphs, inclusive per function; temporary probes around every
`settle`, `install`, snapshot and decode call in `src/_6_eval/_9b_ir_eval.rs`; per-category
statement counters in `ivm-sqlite/src/3_nodes.rs` (`NodesPlan::run`) and the constructor pass loop
in `ivm-sqlite/src/4_engine.rs`. Probes are removed before commit.

Shape of the run: 6 engine sessions (5 macrotime units, 1 comptime), 8 `run_session` calls, 59
settles. Comptime IR: 308 relations, 3357 nodes, 125 outputs, 141 strata, 4959 SQLite CREATE
statements. Macrotime IR: about 105 relations, 1060 nodes, about 1630 CREATE each.

Totals: 831330 SQLite statements across 59 settles, 255 constructor passes, 1809 SCC rounds.
dd time is timely progress tracking (`propagate_pointstamps`, `Tracker::propagate_all`): about
17 ms per epoch over the 3357-node graph, so cost follows epoch count.

## Order of work

1. #1 and #3: make a fold step see the terms minted by the previous step inside the same LetRec,
   so the fold runs in one pass/epoch. Engine-agnostic, in the IR lowering of declared folds.
2. #5, #6, #10 in ivm-sqlite: gate mint registration on the mint's insert count, precompute and
   cache the `count_work` SQL, precompute statement objects.
3. #4 and #9: fewer bootstrap settles.
4. #2: install cost.

## After (sprefa worktree + sqlite_ivm `perf/comptime`)

| mint registration gated on the mint's insert count; constructor scan past the integrated maximum; source fills only for staged relations | ivm-sqlite `3_nodes.rs`, `2_terms.rs` | #5 |

Remaining at 2.7 s (ir:sqlite): six engine installs (comptime about 0.6 s; CREATE cost grows with schema
size through SQLite's `sqlite_schema` rescan per DDL), cold statement preparation (about 0.4 s, one
prepare per distinct statement per session), five macrotime sessions whose IR differs per unit, and
the comptime main settle (7 passes driven by `__ir_plain_inner`, a join on a constructor's argument
columns). ir:dd at 1.4 s: dataflow construction per session and timely progress per epoch.
