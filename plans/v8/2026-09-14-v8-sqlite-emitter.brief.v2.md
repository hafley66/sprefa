## 5. Design (decided)
- Body lowering emits `FROM <first> AS t0 JOIN <next> AS tN ON <equalities against already-joined aliases> ...`. Join order: the first goal as written, then repeatedly the next goal that shares at least one equality with an already-joined alias; a goal with no shared equality joins with `ON 1` and is emitted after every connected goal. Constant filters (`t1."kind" = 5`, `t4."text" = 'const'`, `typeof(...)`) stay in WHERE.
- Everything else in the draft stays: view naming, CTE chaining, recursion with `member` discriminator, NOT EXISTS consumers, GROUP BY aggregates, the `Unsupported` diagnostics list and `expected_diagnostics.json`.
- Test file: keep `check()` as is; replace the single `#[test]` with one `#[test]` per fixture, generated by a macro or written out (`literals_0_float`, ..., `sqlite_emit_4_outside`). Each test asserts its own receipt and prints it on failure. A fixture whose `CREATE VIRTUAL TABLE` exceeds 10 s is a failing test naming the fixture and the DDL, never a wait.
- The extension is not built by the test. The test's existing "no sqlite_ivm extension" panic message stays the instruction.
