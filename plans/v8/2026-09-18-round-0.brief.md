## Goal

A `Once` executor answers its `effect` rows during the compiler fixpoint, so a
program that seeds `(fs.json "todo.json" ?Rows)` compiles with the rows present
and no `--serve` flag. The runtime tick loop and the comptime round loop share
one answer step. `Continuing` executors never run at comptime.
