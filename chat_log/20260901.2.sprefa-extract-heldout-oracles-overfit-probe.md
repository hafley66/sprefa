  Real reclaim was 46+38 merged worktrees plus 4 cargo cleans (v6/sprefa-store/target = 24 GiB).

  edges. Absolute is a strict superset (9 import pairs, 3 call pairs, zero the other way).
  Found because a sonnet agent's numbers BEAT the coordinator's key.

  bench/mod.rs `enumerate()` reimplements the walk. It also causes 106 by telling users to
  `find`, which yields relative paths.

  polyglot indexer pick, and (earlier) the feature-absent ratchet.
