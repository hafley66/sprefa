## Key Insights
- Two trait seams: OperatorDef (compile, op maker, lower() is the bridge) vs Component (runtime, 3 tiers render/render_batch/dispatch, inner->outer defaults).
- render/render_batch must be pure (Church-Rosser => memo/replay); dispatch is the only impure tier; Node::Yield = reified delimited continuation, dispatch = effect handler (algebraic effects / free monad).
- Support edge = JTMS justification / provenance-semiring annotation: Row<-input. Emit it => facts auto-retract; omit => monotone/sticky. It is the opt-in to differential correctness.
- DD constant-RSS lever is compaction/frontier advancement (quotient history group below frontier), then tiering for cold; user's Support-recompute model is the Adapton/Salsa dual (less RAM, more recompute).
- run_to_quiescence = Kleene iteration of T_P to lfp; IterationCap = unsound truncation. Negation breaks monotonicity; stratification (level map, no negative-edge cycle, decidable topo-sort) restores a unique perfect model. This closes both the recursion risk and the dependency-crawl limitation.
- Two open soundness holes: (1) purity is promise not type; (2) fixed point capped not stratified.
- Rosetta: content hash=alpha key; ${X}/rule=beta; passthrough=eta; Gen re-fire=ΔT_P/semi-naive=DD first-order; expand+queue=CEK machine.
