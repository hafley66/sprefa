## Key Insights
- **SprfPath IS the entity id** — captured framing for blast-radius analysis (13.5). Because pipelines program the match, any capture has a stable address.
- **sh[] is the bulking primitive** — first way out of pure surface; temporal ops ride on top once watch sources exist.
- **Merge is already syntactic** in sprefa via ForkArm reconvergence; rxjs merge() doesn't need a new op, just the path-tagging we have.
- **Spike cards = design sessions** with the user. Implementation must not start without confirming direction. Encoded in CLAUDE.md.
- **bd `close <id>` needs `-r "reason"`** — positional reason arg is treated as second ID, fails silently-ish. Documented in reference_beads_tracker memory.
- **Safety taxonomy**: idempotent byte-range writes over comment_scope are user-visible as diffs (safe). PR/API side effects need approval gates (unsafe). Encode per-effect at the effect type, not at the call site.
- **Reusing v2 impls is valid** — ast-grep/json/md P1 port cards unblocked from pattern-trait-surface dep since v2 impls port straight across.
