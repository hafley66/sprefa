## Key Insights
- **RA emits local monikers as opaque IDs** (`local 0`, `local 1`...). Source
  name lives in `SymbolInformation.display_name` per-document on `doc.symbols`.
  Required a join; the initial extraction grabbed numeric IDs.
- **`debug = 0` in Cargo.toml profile** took clean build from swap-thrash
  (ongoing for an hour, 4.5M swapins) to 24 seconds on 16GB box. debuginfo
  generation dominates rustc memory. -j 4 caps parallelism.
- **N-rater card sort ARI 0.65** (6 cold-reads): the cold-read decomposition
  is reproducible, not noise. 10 unanimous consensus clusters.
- **dl field-coverage vs every rater: ARI ~0.04.** Structural and semantic
  are orthogonal axes; this is the empirical resolution of the prior
  paradox.
- **missing-type recipe thresholds**: length >= 10 + fns >= 5 + files >= 3
  gives 20 hits, zero noise. `label_span` at 96/98 is near-universal.
- **touches_* triple**: 3 fns have all 3, 7 have at least 2. Real
  context-object signal (TouchMask struct candidate).
- **derived_* pair**: 3 of 7 fns have both. Real context-object signal.
- **TurboMQ call-only** would rank db_only low (44 methods call self.db not
  each other); need field-aware variant for fair scoring.
- **`Engine#<field>` moniker shape** already isolable in dl; scip_fn_edge
  emits field references because it emits every referenced symbol.
- **dl limitations confirmed**: no `sum` aggregate (needed for TurboMQ);
  regex backref for "not already a global" doesn't work cleanly; tuple
  binds (`p = (a, b)`) fail in head.
- **MCL ≠ MCMC**: Wikipedia's "Markov clustering" redirect is wrong; MCL
  is van Dongen 2000 (flow simulation), MCMC is stochastic sampling.
