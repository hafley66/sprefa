## Architecture decisions (load-bearing)
- **Sink classification.** A `repo`-headed rule is excluded from `derived_rels`
  so `rebuild_derived` (which does `DELETE FROM rel`) never wipes the
  engine-emitted registered set. Mirrors `gen` (Item::Gen). The hard
  source-vs-derived bail (engine.rs:1487) is why a builtin can't be user-derived.
- **1-tick latency for data-driven scan + repo-sink.** The coord/pull relation is
  derived AFTER source; the scan/pull reads LAST tick's relation. Avoids a
  source→derived→source fixpoint rewrite (the true big-rock). Foreground primes
  silently; daemon converges in 2.
- **tick_paths cliffs.** Any program with a data-driven scan OR repo-sink OR a
  non-self changed path (#6a) full-ticks. Reactivity gained, incrementality lost
  for those. Mitigation later: a digest on the coord relation.
- **Origin-based resolution (#4b).** No AST rewriting, no daemon file-slug
  coupling — the rule carries its source file, and resolve_scan_bindings +
  resolve_write_full prefer its `.git` ancestor. Foreground unchanged (origin's
  ancestor == self.root).
