- ast-grep's `$$$` multi-node prefix is OUTSIDE the host's `${...}` carveout. Host grammar treats lone `$` as opaque slot_punct; ast op recognizes outer-prefix `$$${VAR?}`. Same `${VAR?}` carveout serves single-node ast metavar AND term_ref AND tag arg shape.
