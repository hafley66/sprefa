- boop = the database (`~/.agent/boop.db`) + events. Reacting is dl6's job. boop never compiles/boots engines.
- concatmap is ONE operator: `session_turns$.pipe(bufferWhile(same role), pairwise(), concatMap(bundle => resident.send(bundle)))`. Resident small model owns a live `seq.d2` and folds each (ai_run, user_run) bundle in; its replies are its own session's turns; "handled" is a query.
