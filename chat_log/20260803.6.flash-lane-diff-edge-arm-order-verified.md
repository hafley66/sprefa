## Key Insights
- The silent case is silent BY CONSTRUCTION: analyze.pl:1331 only inspects keyed
  heads, so a log head is never pairwise-compared. That is the one-line
  explanation of the whole finding.
- keep(all) makes arm order visible in the delta SEQUENCE only; keep(count(N))
  makes it decide the SURVIVOR. Every checked-in same-head log arm is keep(all),
  which is why the tree is green while the hole is real.

  reorder is already observable in emitted artifacts even where the final state
  is not.

  gen_served/) is invisible to worktree lanes. Any brief that cites a probe

  null and the weaker model will explain it away.
