## FINAL VERDICT: adopt no graph library. Own the CSR.

The win in every measurement was the MEMORY LAYOUT, never the library. With
storage held constant sprefa's own tarjan BEATS petgraph:

| graph | tarjan/Vec<Vec<u32>> | tarjan/DualCsr | kosaraju/DualCsr |
|---|---|---|---|
| rel_df_edge | 19.5ms | 9.9ms | 14.2ms |
| rel_flow_edge | 18.3ms | 9.9ms | 15.0ms |
| synth 1M/2M | 196.8ms | 102.7ms | 119.6ms |

Round 2's "petgraph 1.47x faster" was a STORAGE CONFOUND: it benchmarked
tarjan(&Vec<Vec<u32>>) vs kosaraju(&DualCsr) and credited the delta to petgraph.

Rejected: petgraph, ultragraph, GraphBLAS, CozoDB, LadybugDB.
No option exists that runs compiled graph algorithms over non-resident data.
The search is CLOSED, not unfinished.

## Key Insights

**D-G1 no library.** Storage-held-constant, petgraph is 1.16x-1.43x SLOWER.

**D-G2 CSR, owned.** Fill from `ORDER BY source, target`. Never build-then-compress
(ultragraph freeze peaks 1.85x, structural to the shape). v5 cost is 16V+8E because
node ids are i64 symbol hashes at density ~3e-14 needing a dictionary; v6 D1
surrogate keys DELETE that term -> 8E + dense offsets. 1.56GB extrapolation was
wrong; v5 real is 3.44GB.

**D-G3 tier keys on MEAN REACH, not graph size.** Build-vs-CTE crossover spans
1.5 to 297 queries. rel_df_edge and rel_flow_edge are near-identical in size with
crossovers 198x apart.

**D-G4 CTEs cover traversal, 3 limits.** 12/12 walk.rs + 5 edge cases + 0/400
differential fuzz. Limits: (1) multi-seed from a TABLE loses the index seek,
7.02ms vs 0.24ms, and that is the only shape multi_source_walk has; (2) cost
tracks reached EDGES not nodes, 0.31ms at out-degree 3 vs 5.99ms at 299;
(3) reverse index mandatory, 4,000x.

**D-G5 SCC + count_pairs stay in Rust.** SQL FB-SCC is QUADRATIC in peel-core size
(fit across 40x, <4% residuals); rel_flow_edge did not finish in 25 min.
count_pairs published ~4s was a rel_df_edge number; rel_flow_edge extrapolates to
4.3 HOURS (3,852x miss).

**D-G6 depth premise was wrong by 3 orders of magnitude.** Brief assumed 1,000,000;
real max DFS depth is 690 vs petgraph's 65,420 overflow threshold (95x margin).
All stack-safety concern from rounds 1-2 was hypothetical.

**THE SAMPLING ERROR.** rel_df_edge is 1 of 18 non-empty edge relations and the
FRIENDLIEST (max SCC 6, mean reach 5.35). rel_flow_edge, largest at 371,404 edges,
has a 22,257-node SCC and mean reach 15,653. Eccentricity: df_edge max 34,
flow_edge >=112 with p99 79.

**LadybugDB scc is SILENTLY WRONG.** Merged two vertices with a one-direction edge
into an SCC on real data; 65% off on a cyclic synthetic. Effective maxIterations is
bit-identical to 20 while DOCUMENTED default is 100; non-convergence returns a
partial partition WITH NO ERROR. scc_ko is exact but 6.3x slower than in-memory
tarjan. Buffer pool cannot spill algorithm state: 16-24GB needed at 150M nodes.
~200 edges/sec incremental, full rebuild wins past 43 edges.

**GraphBLAS.** LAGraph_scc was installed on this machine DURING the first
evaluation, one `nm | grep` away. Correct, and costs 158.4s at depth 4,000 while a
283k-node random graph takes 0.15s. Depth-fatal, size-cheap. Its 12-23x transpose
was a harness artifact (1.21x with GrB_Matrix_wait, 0.99x storing A' BY_COL).
NonCommercial blocker dissolves: 443-line shim, 56 decls. Clean build 4.16s not 137s.

**Prior art: none.** 46 logged queries. No published SQL SCC anywhere. Vertica's
canonical survey omits SCC. DuckDB USING KEY SIGMOD 2025 and Hirn & Grust CIDR 2023
both score 0 on "SCC"/"Tarjan"/"strongly connected". Souffle runs Gabow on the RULE
graph in C++ for stratification, never exposed. Zero petgraph-over-disk prior art.

**Method law earned:** a measurement over one instance of a family is a HYPOTHESIS
about the family. Name the sample or the number reads as general.
