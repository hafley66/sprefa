## Key Insights
- The four-layer cake (DSLs / pipes / facts / generations) is the cleanest framing of what sprefa actually is. Composition rules differ per layer; cross-layer enforcement at lower-time gives clean diagnostics.
- Tick lifecycle = React render+flush. Render phase stages effects (sh, write_*, lsp[…]); flush phase resolves them after generation seals. Same primitive from a different angle.
- Generations as runtime primitive is NOT primarily for time-travel queries — it's the invariant that makes determinism, termination, stable pauses, and side-effect ordering fall out for free. Time-travel is gravy.
- Anti-join soundness reduces to Datalog stratification adapted to streams: lower-time analysis ensures writers complete before readers see negation; runtime RAII makes sealing actually happen.
- Cursor-collapse (captures HashMap + addressing-as-captures) is the prerequisite to write architecture. Source ops become uniform; pattern ops unchanged; optimizer hides the pre-filter perf concern.
- The user's "am I just reinventing my own shell" question: yes, with three additions (typed captures flowing, effect cache, reactive subjects). LSP-as-runtime amplifies the third. Worth eight months IF the headline (drift-as-you-type) lands.
- "carveout" stays as canonical term for `${...}` parser re-entry. Insect glaive / floor-is-lava coded.
- v1/v2/chat_log/memory all archived to ~/projects/sprefa-archive-20260428/ (9.3GB v2 alone). Workspace post-clearing is lean: AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, editors/, justfile, v3/.
- The proto-drift demo in VS Code is the headline. ~3 weeks of focused work after cleanup baseline. If it lands and feels good, eight months pay back.
- Stale sprefa-server daemon at ~/.cache/sprefa/server.json is a recurring landmine; pkill + rm before testing pipeline-crate ABI changes. Documented in CLAUDE.md.
- Withhold opinions until asked. Prior tendency to produce dispatch tables / "my pick" / pros-cons triggered "im tired of your fucking opinions."
- Auto-seal RAII is the runtime expression of structured concurrency for Subjects. When all writer frames drop, the Bag's generation seals; anti-join readers wake.
- The eventual collapse vision (TERM as next(...) sugar, pipes-as-values, rules-as-relations) is research-flavored but not blocking the demo.
