language design as a fixpoint on a reduction graph

Nodes = ideas. An edge A → B means “A enables B” (B is expressible using A). Roots with no in-edge are primitives. The question we keep asking — can we express X with none of the previous ideas? — is just: does X’s dependency closure bottom out only in accepted primitives? Click any node to crawl its closure and get the verdict.
