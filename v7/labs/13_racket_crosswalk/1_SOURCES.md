## Runnable examples recorded, not run

The Datalog manual gives a recursive `#lang datalog/sexp` path example, including facts and two `path` clauses: [example](https://docs.racket-lang.org/datalog/Parenthetical_Datalog_Module_Language.html). Racklog documents `%which`, `%more`, `%=` and `use-occurs-check?`: [examples](https://docs.racket-lang.org/racklog/predicates.html) and [occurs-check example](https://docs.racket-lang.org/racklog/unification.html). Rosette documents `#lang rosette/safe` and `raco pkg install rosette`: [example](https://docs.racket-lang.org/rosette-guide/ch_getting-started.html). `raco exe` documents `raco exe --gui hello.rkt`: [example](https://docs.racket-lang.org/raco/exe.html).

These are upstream examples. Their results, performance, supported solver backends, executable size, dynamic dependencies, and behavior on the shared cyclic fixture are unresolved locally.
