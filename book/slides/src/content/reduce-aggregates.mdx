## !!steps Groups

```sql !
-- !focus(2)
SELECT clerk, SUM(amount)
FROM sales
GROUP BY clerk;
```

## !!steps Aggregate

```sql !
-- !focus(1:2)
sum['ada'] = 4 + 6 = 10
sum['bea'] = 3
```
